Oppenheimer (2023)

⭐ 8.5/10 • 3h 0m • R
Drama History Thriller

"The world forever changes."

Overview

The story of American scientist J. Robert Oppenheimer and his role in the development of the atomic bomb. Based on the Pulitzer Prize-winning book "American Prometheus" by Kai Bird and Martin J. Sherwin, the film chronicles Oppenheimer's journey from a brilliant young physicist to the leader of the Manhattan Project, and the moral complexities he faced as the "father of the atomic bomb."
